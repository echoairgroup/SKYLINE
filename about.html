Important limitations
BEFORE CONNECTING REAL OPERATIONS

This release is a static front-end demonstration. It has no backend, authentication, database, ADS-B provider, airline schedule feed or real aircraft telemetry.

A production live-operations product requires a trusted data provider, backend services, security controls, monitoring, terms of use and validation before operational use.
